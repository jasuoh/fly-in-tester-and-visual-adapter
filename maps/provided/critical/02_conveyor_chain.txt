# Critical 02: Conveyor chain
#
# A single-file tunnel of five max_drones=1 zones is the ONLY path for
# 8 drones. The rule "drones moving out of a zone free up capacity for
# that same turn" is what makes this solvable efficiently: once the
# tunnel is full, the whole line of drones can advance one step every
# turn, like a train, instead of needing two turns per hop (one to
# vacate, one to enter).
#
# Watch out for: an implementation that evaluates "can I move in" and
# "did someone move out" as two separate turns instead of one atomic
# turn will roughly double the turn count on this map. Compare your
# result against (tunnel length + nb_drones - 1) turns.
